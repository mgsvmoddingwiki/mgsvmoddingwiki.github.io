[*Metal Gear Solid V: Ground Zeroes*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_Gear_Solid_V:_Ground_Zeroes) is the first of the two MGSV games using Fox Engine and shares similar assets.

Its Steam app ID is `311340` and *The Phantom Pain*'s save files are stored in that same directory (see [Save files](/Save_files) for more about this).
